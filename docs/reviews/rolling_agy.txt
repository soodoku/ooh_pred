In [scripts/rolling.R](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/rolling.R#L45-L47), the 80/20 split is drawn from `members` (`member_mob.txt`), and [`landmark_snapshot()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/rolling.R#L34) filters `cans` to `member_id %in% members`. Consequently, that 1 member missing from `member_mob.txt` is excluded from both `training_members` and `testing_members` in the rolling extension, whereas in [ms/main.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd#L91-L98) Table 1 (`tabs/cohort.csv`) "Excluded without pre-origin CANS" is computed as `nrow(members) - nrow(features)`, which understates `members` without CANS by 1 because 1 row of `features` is not in `members`.
   - **Impact:** Negligible numerically (1 person with no admission records in `admission.txt`), and causes zero leakage, but worth noting if exact set-partitioning against `member_mob.txt` is asserted.

### Verification of Audit Focus Areas

- **Time and Person Leakage:**
  - **Person separation:** `rsample::initial_split(dplyr::select(members, member_id), prop = 0.8)` in [scripts/rolling.R](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/rolling.R#L45-L48) partitions members once; disjointness is asserted globally (`stopifnot`) and verified at every window (`overlapping_members == 0`).
  - **Temporal separation:** In [`rolling_windows()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/rolling.R#L1-L28), `distance <- horizon + gap + label_delay` (`label_delay = 30L`), so `train_start = target_start - horizon - gap - 30`, `train_end = train_start + horizon = cutoff - 30`, and `stopifnot(all(windows$train_end <= windows$cutoff - label_delay))` holds across all 27 windows.
  - **Internal fold isolation:** Each snapshot has at most one row per member (`stopifnot(!anyDuplicated(features$member_id))`). Inside [`fit_lasso()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/models.R#L64-L76), [`prepare_matrix()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/models.R#L1-L38) fits the 0.5%-or-25-member vocabulary threshold, missingness indicators, median imputation, and zero-variance filtering strictly on the internal fold's development subset `training[development, ]`.
- **Availability Dates:**
  - **CANS:** [`landmark_snapshot()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/rolling.R#L34-L35) requires `finish >= start, finish < cutoff, submitted < cutoff, created < cutoff`.
  - **Prior admissions:** [`landmark_snapshot()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/rolling.R#L46) requires `admission_date >= start, admission_date < cutoff, created_date < cutoff`. In [scripts/descriptive.R](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/descriptive.R#L61), pre-origin prior placements likewise require `admission_date < origin, created_date < origin`.
  - **Training vs. test labels:** [`landmark_labels()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/rolling.R#L63-L68) enforces `admission_date >= start, admission_date < end, created_date < available_before`, called with `available_before = window$cutoff` for training labels ([scripts/rolling.R](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/rolling.R#L79-L82)) and unavailed (`9999-01-01`) for test labels, with the difference explicitly tracked in `late_training_positives` ([scripts/rolling.R](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/rolling.R#L116-L119)) and reported in [ms/supplement.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/supplement.Rmd#L132-L147).
  - **Records:** Diagnoses (`createdate < cutoff`) and medications (`createddate < cutoff`) use creation dates; eligibility uses `startdate < cutoff`, and [ms/supplement.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/supplement.Rmd#L82) and [ms/main.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd#L132) explicitly state that eligibility entry dates and historical record update versions (`updatedate > createdate` in 112,788 diagnosis rows) and later demographic snapshots are unavailable.
- **Horizon Arithmetic & Feasibility:**
  - All 5 settings (`rolling_main`, `history_180`, `gap_zero`, `outcome_90`, `outcome_365`) filter to `train_cutoff - lookback >= source_start` (`2012-11-14`, first medication creation date) and `target_end <= source_end` (`2016-11-03`, day after last recorded admission `2016-11-02`). This yields 6 quarterly dates for the first four settings and 3 shared dates (`2015-04-01`, `2015-07-01`, `2015-10-01`) for `outcome_365` (27 total windows).
  - Lead-gap admissions (`[cutoff, target_start)`) are excluded from both predictors (`< cutoff`) and target outcomes (`>= target_start`), and youth with gap admissions are not conditioned out of the risk set.
- **Source-Completeness Claims:**
  - Neither [ms/main.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd#L100-L102) nor [ms/supplement.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/supplement.Rmd#L86-L87) claims that the unobserved extraction query is known or that a follow-up buffer guarantees complete ascertainment. [ms/supplement.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/supplement.Rmd#L167-L196) reports empirical admission starts, valid durations, median/90th-percentile stays by year, and days remaining to the last discharge while stating that every supplied admission has a discharge date, that this does not prove how the extract was queried, and that no arbitrary buffer can recover absent placements.
- **Accurate Medication Descriptions & Correct Denominators:**

  - Denominators in `tabs/feature_descriptive.csv` and `tabs/medication_descriptive.csv` are all cohort youth without placement (`n_0 = 14,425`) and with placement (`n_1 = 907`), summing to `15,332`.
  - [docs/medication_display.csv](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/docs/medication_display.csv) and [ms/main.Rmd](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd#L173-L176) accurately label the abbreviated/truncated source strings (e.g., `Risperdal (dose truncated)` for `risperdal 0.5 m`, `Seroquel (100 mg/1 tablet entry)` for `seroquel quetiapine fumarate 100 mg/1 oral tablet,`) and note that records do not establish active use, adherence, or harmonized drugs/doses.
