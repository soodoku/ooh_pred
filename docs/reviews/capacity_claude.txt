## Correctness defects

1. **The abstract overgeneralises the 2015 result** (`ms/main.Rmd:83`). It says "A calendar-year 2015 analysis also finds higher yield than these comparators." That is true only at k=100 (26 vs 18 and 14), and the gain over the history rule there is 8 (95% CI −2 to 16). At k=50 the history rule beats the full model (12 vs 10), and the CANS-and-demographics model ties it. The sentence needs the capacity and the uncertainty.

2. **The supplement's frozen-score sentence names the wrong ranking and rests on one capacity** (`ms/supplement.Rmd:111`, fed by `scripts/targeting.R:60–71`).
   - "The earlier endpoint therefore retains useful ranking information" credits the wrong score. The ranking that retains information is the longer-window score, held fixed and judged on 2015 admissions (21 vs 4.9 random).
   - The horizon comparison is computed only at k=100, where refitting gains 5 (−2 to 9). At k=200 and k=500 the frozen long-window score finds more 2015 events than the refitted one-year model (47 vs 43, 77 vs 76). These are my point counts; I did not bootstrap them. So refitting to the 2015 endpoint gives no consistent advantage.
   - Either report all four capacities or state that the comparison is k=100 only.

3. **"Incremental value over the history rule is uncertain" is unqualified in the abstract** (`ms/main.Rmd:83`). It holds at k=50 (0, −7 to 8) and k=100 (5, −5 to 15). At k=200 and k=500 the intervals exclude zero: 18 (5 to 29) and 16 (2 to 31). The conclusion at `main.Rmd:400` is correctly scoped to "capacity to assess 100 youth"; the abstract should be too.
