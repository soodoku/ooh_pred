- **Location:** [`scripts/prepare.R`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/prepare.R#L25-L30) and [`R/models.R`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/models.R#L24-L32) (`prepare_matrix()`).
- **Evidence:**
  1. In [`scripts/prepare.R`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/scripts/prepare.R#L25-L30), `white` and `black` are both set to `NA_real_` under the identical condition `is.na(race) | race %in% c("UNKNOWN", "DECLINED")` (`1,218` youth, `7.94%`). Consequently, [`prepare_matrix()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/models.R#L25-L28) creates two identical columns (`identical(pm_main$train[, "white_missing"], pm_main$train[, "black_missing"]) == TRUE` in both train and test). Because L1/coordinate descent in `glmnet` handles duplicate columns without changing predictions (putting the weight on `white_missing` and `0` or `-1.6e-15` on `black_missing`), fitted predictions are unaffected, though one indicator is redundant.

In [`prepare_matrix()`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/R/models.R#L25-L28), `if (anyNA(train[[column]]))` creates `<column>_missing` only when the training fold has at least one `NA`. In the actual dataset, `sum(sapply(test_f, anyNA) & !sapply(train_f, anyNA)) == 0` under both `main` and `special_missing`, so this edge case never triggers in practice (and `test[[column]]` is still safely median-imputed at line 31).

## 4. Remaining Scientific Limitations

All of the following substantive limitations are inherent to the supplied PerformCare extract and are **already accurately disclosed** in [`ms/main.Rmd`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd):

1. **Retrospective, outcome-dependent cohort construction:** The supplied 22,282-member extract was sampled partly using 2014–2015 OOH admissions and CMO enrollment, overlapping the post-January 1, 2015 prediction window ([`ms/main.Rmd`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd#L77-L79)). Held-out evaluation measures discrimination within this selected historical cohort (`N = 15,332` with pre-origin CANS), not prospective transportability to anenumerated service population at the forecast origin.
2. **Incomplete outcome ascertainment and facility exclusions:** Every record in `data/admission.txt` has a discharge date, so ongoing stays at extraction may be omitted (`ms/main.Rmd:88–90`), and developmental disability, substance use, group/treatment home, detox, and SJI settings are excluded (`ms/main.Rmd:77`).
3. **Lack of historical snapshot versioning:** `member_mob.txt` is a single undated snapshot, `eligibility.txt` provides `startdate` without system entry dates, and `74` pre-origin diagnosis rows (`0.0086%`, affecting `15` cohort youth) have `updatedate >= 2015-01-01` (`ms/main.Rmd:118–120`).
4. **Estimand boundaries (repeated starts vs. clinical readmission; window occupancy vs. episode length):** `repeat_placement` measures $\ge 2$ distinct admission start dates in the fixed window (leaving `308` cohort youth with 1 post-origin start and prior pre-2015 placements coded `0`, and without distinguishing facility transfers from post-discharge returns), while `recorded_days` measures clipped window occupancy including carryovers (`ms/main.Rmd:100–105`).
5. **Conditional uncertainty and rare-event precision:** Bootstrap and exact binomial intervals condition on the fitted model and split, and cannot account for household/provider clustering or cohort-selection bias (`ms/main.Rmd:157, 389`). At the Youden threshold, rare-event precision remains low (`12.2%` for placement; `2.7%` for multiple placements), and for multiple placements the Brier skill over a constant training-prevalence prediction is near zero (`0.7%`, 95% CI `-2.2%` to `2.9%`, [`ms/main.Rmd`](file:///Users/soodoku/Documents/GitHub/soodoku/ooh_pred/ms/main.Rmd#L201)).
